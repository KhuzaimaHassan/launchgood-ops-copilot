# Synthetic Documentation Review Policy

## Evidence requirement

Campaigns making a specific medical or emergency claim should provide supporting documentation when requested by a reviewer. The reviewer may request additional material when the available evidence is incomplete.

## Human review boundary

Missing documentation should trigger human review. This policy does not authorize an automated suspension, refund, or external communication.
